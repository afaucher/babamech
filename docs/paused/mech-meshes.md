## Numbers that will shift with the walk speed change
The main session is raising the default walk speed from 6 to about 12 m/s and retuning the gait. These were set or
measured at walk 6 / run 13 m/s, mech scale 2, stance 1.35, and must be rechecked after that lands:
- Strafe speed: `strafe_scale` 0.6, so 3.6 m/s walking and 7.8 m/s running. At 12 m/s walk the walking strafe
  becomes 7.2 m/s.
- The shuffle limit: with a 2.7 m stance and 1.3 m `foot_clearance` the sideways lead is capped at 0.7 m per step,
  and steps shorten to no less than `min_step_time` 0.16 s. By my estimate (not measured) the feet keep up to about
  5 m/s sideways before they trail the hips; 7.8 m/s only passed the tests with overlapping steps.
- Test thresholds in `test_gait_strafe_step.gd` (gap >= 1.25 m, side > 0.3 m, lag < 3.0 m at 3.6 and 7.8 m/s) and
  `test_ring_step.gd` (gap >= 1.2 m, side > 0.2 m) assume those speeds.
- Step-up is geometric, not speed based, so it should not move unless leg lengths or crouch change: foot lift
  1.44 m, max step-up 1.30 m (`step_margin` 0.9), hip height about 4.3 m; both figures worked out by hand from the leg
  lengths. `climb_speed` 6 m/s was matched to the old walk speed, so climbing a curb at 12 m/s will slow the mech
  more than before.
